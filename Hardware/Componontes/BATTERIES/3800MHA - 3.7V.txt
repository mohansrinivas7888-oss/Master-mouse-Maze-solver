OselTech 18650 3800mAh 3.7V Li-Ion Rechargeable Battery
The OselTech 18650 is a lithium-ion rechargeable cell with a 3800mAh capacity. This cell operates at a 3.7V nominal voltage. The unit features a flat top terminal configuration. It supports up to 1000 charge cycles under standard operating conditions. The operational temperature range is -20°C to +85°C. This is an unprotected cell. External protection circuitry is required for safety in specific applications. The physical dimensions are 18mm in diameter and 65mm in height.
