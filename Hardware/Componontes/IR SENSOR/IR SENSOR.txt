The "Flying Fish" IR infrared obstacle and proximity sensor module uses an active infrared emitter and photodiode pair managed by a voltage comparator


Technical Specifications

• Operating Voltage: 3.3V to 5V DC
• Detection Range: 2 cm to 30 cm (adjustable via onboard potentiometer)
• Detection Angle: 35°
• Output Type: Digital (LOW when an obstacle is detected, HIGH otherwise)
• Comparator IC: LM393
• Operating Current: ~20 mA
• Dimensions: Approx. 45 mm x 13 mm x 8 mm


Pin Configuration

• VCC: 3.3V – 5V power supply input
• GND: Ground connection
• OUT: Digital signal output (goes low in the presence of a reflected obstacle)


Onboard Indicators & Controls

• Power LED: Illuminates when power is applied to the module.
• Obstacle/Status LED: Illuminates when an object is detected within range.
• Potentiometer: Turn clockwise to increase detection distance; turn counter-clockwise to decrease detection distance
