## 📖 Overview
The **OselTech 18650** is a high-capacity lithium-ion rechargeable cell designed for portable electronics and robotics applications (such as Micromouse maze solvers). Operating at a nominal voltage of **3.7V** with a **3800mAh** capacity, it features a standard 18650 form factor (`18mm × 65mm`) and a flat-top terminal configuration. Under standard operating conditions, it supports up to **1,000 charge-discharge cycles**.

> [!WARNING]
> **SAFETY & PROTECTION NOTICE (UNPROTECTED CELL)**  
> This battery **does not** contain a built-in Battery Management System (BMS). External protection circuitry (such as a 1S/2S BMS module) or microcontroller ADC voltage monitoring is strictly required to prevent:
> * **Overcharging:** Voltage exceeding `4.2V`
> * **Over-discharging:** Voltage dropping below `3.0V`
> * **Short circuits & thermal runaway**
