       OSELTECH 18650 3800mAh 3.7V LI-ION RECHARGEABLE BATTERY

OVERVIEW
--------
The OselTech 18650 is a high-capacity lithium-ion rechargeable cell 
designed for portable electronics and robotics applications. Operating 
at a nominal voltage of 3.7V with a 3800mAh capacity, it features a 
standard 18650 form factor (18mm x 65mm) and a flat-top terminal 
configuration. Under standard operating conditions, it supports up to 
1,000 charge-discharge cycles.

[!] SAFETY & PROTECTION NOTICE:
This is an UNPROTECTED cell. It does not contain a built-in Battery 
Management System (BMS). External protection circuitry or software 
voltage monitoring is strictly required to prevent overcharging (>4.2V), 
over-discharging (<3.0V), and short circuits.


TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
------------------------
* Brand                 : OselTech
* Battery Chemistry     : Lithium-Ion (Li-Ion)
* Form Factor           : 18650
* Nominal Capacity      : 3800 mAh
* Nominal Voltage       : 3.7 V
* Full Charge Voltage   : 4.2 V
* Cutoff Voltage        : 3.0 V
* Terminal Type         : Flat Top
* Protection Circuit    : None (Unprotected Cell)
* Cycle Life            : Up to 1,000 charge cycles
* Operating Temperature : -20°C to +85°C
* Physical Dimensions   : 18 mm (Diameter) x 65 mm (Height)
