The HC-SR04 is a low-cost, non-contact distance and proximity measurement sensor that uses 40 kHz ultrasonic sound waves to detect objects between 2 cm and 400 cm.

Key Specifications & Features

• Measurement Range: 2 cm to 400 cm (up to 4.5m depending on the revision)
• Accuracy: Approximately 3 mm to 3.0 mm
• Operating Voltage: DC 5V (some newer versions support 3.3V logic)
• Operating Current: 15 mA
• Measurement Angle: ~15° to 30°
• Operating Frequency: 40 kHz

Pin Configuration (4 Pins)

• VCC: Connects to 5V power supply
• Trig (Trigger): Input pin; receives a 10 µs HIGH TTL pulse to start the ultrasonic burst
• Echo: Output pin; goes HIGH during signal transmission and stays high until the reflected echo is received
• GND: System ground
